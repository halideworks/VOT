# Patent and Provenance Policy

This document is not legal advice and does not modify the AGPL-3.0-only license.

Contributors must disclose known patent claims they own or control that are
necessarily infringed by their contribution. Contributions must be original or
derived from sources whose licenses permit inclusion. Proprietary source,
decompiled logic, leaked specifications, and undocumented protocol cloning are
forbidden.

Adding or materially changing a hash, proof, compression, congestion-control,
FEC, coding, authentication, or cryptographic algorithm requires:

1. an accepted ADR;
2. primary-source and prior-art references;
3. dependency and license review;
4. contributor provenance attestation; and
5. legal review when a known encumbrance or commercial-product comparison is in
   scope.

`PRIOR_ART.md` is an engineering record. It is not a patent clearance,
freedom-to-operate opinion, or exhaustive search.
